# Thử nghiệm và nhân rộng góc nội dung

Start from product facts, research evidence, the audience problem and actual offer. Use persistent angle IDs and separate variants for hook, proof, CTA and format. One experiment should change one main variable. For Sakura distinguish multipurpose cleaner from fabric spray; do not interchange bottle or use cases.

Produce concise Vietnamese hooks, 4–6-slide carousel outlines, captions, disclosure needs and a measurable hypothesis. A 100-post target is a delivery goal, not authorization to spam or clone the same creative. Start with a diverse pilot and use measured results before expanding the remaining backlog. A queue record must distinguish idea, asset-ready, scheduled and verified-published states.

Compare same-product/same-format posts at comparable post ages, with organic and paid distribution separated. Prefer leads/orders when actually measured; otherwise report engagement as a proxy. Do not call an angle a proven winner from one viral post. Require baseline and sample-size checks. The repo's 3-post/500-view/20%-uplift defaults are operating heuristics, not significance tests.

When a signal is credible, reserve roughly 70% of the next test batch for variations on promising angles and 30% for exploration, subject to user cadence. Reassess after each batch. Preserve weak results and explain confounders rather than deleting them.
