# Speech-led Sakura edits

Use this for narrated product stories made from the supplied footage. It addresses the observed weak pattern: four fixed three-second clips, repeated large headings, a product-poster intro and a long full-poster tail. A new heading or filter does not fix a missing visual story.

Inspect source actions before choosing the script or shot. Bind each selected interval to its actual hash and reviewed range. Describe what changes in the viewer's understanding, what the next shot earns, and what the assets cannot show. Use motion footage for household context and authentic imagery for product identity. Keep illustrative actors/scenes separate from customer testimony and product demonstrations.

Preserve the exact approved narration. Read its phrase timing receipt and raw ASR. Save an edit plan whose shot boundaries use actual phrase anchors, allowing frame rounding; keep the timing uncertainty explicit. Choose a shot's length for the action and thought. One action may continue across several caption cues. A scene change may earn a direct cut, match cut or gentle transition; an effect on every beat is not a requirement.

Start on the recognizable problem or useful action. Keep the first frame meaningful with sound off. Use typography for a relationship, decision or emphasis that the image needs. Captions carry the speech; an additional full-sentence heading often duplicates it. Keep hands, faces and the relevant object visible. Review the encoded opening and each cut in motion.

The repository's `scripts/render-scale-video-preview.mjs --edit-plan ABS_PLAN` reuses the existing registered-intent, grounding, approved-voice and caption workflow. `src/story-edit.mjs` renders a frame-bound plan with reviewed portrait or source-region framing, full-scene foregrounds when required, short authentic product crops and a context-led CTA. Portrait/region choices bind actual contact-sheet bytes; inspect the crop and record the judgment before using it. Do not override a source that needs full-scene framing. A failed portrait crop may need a wider source region, rather than smaller action inside a generic blurred frame. This is a bounded rendering option, not implementation of every effect described by upstream Herk. Check its actual output before selecting it for another story.

The plan binds intent, exact WAV/script, authentic reference, actual timing receipt and a same-audio baseline receipt. Each footage shot supplies a reviewed source ID/hash/start; product crops stay within the real reference. Frame coverage is continuous. Per-shot caption positions move each actual phrase above important hands/objects without changing its text or times; inspect cues that cross a cut. The current short-story mode has technical duration/shot bounds and copies AAC packets unchanged from the same-narration baseline. The caption pass also verifies audio-stream copying. These checks establish media integrity; they do not establish creative quality or word-perfect timing.

Save DESIGN.md before rendering. After rendering, inspect the opening, source-action continuity, every transition, product crops, subtitle position and final CTA. Keep a moving before/after comparison when it helps evaluate the correction. Record what was actually viewed or heard. Use the existing exact listening approval for visual-only changes; ask for a new one only when narration changes. Native music selection/mix and platform preflight still happen separately, with one submission owner.
