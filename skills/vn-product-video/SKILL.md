## Build the story from usable footage

Inspect the actual source intervals first: visible action, faces/hands, framing, source hashes and what the footage can honestly establish. Supplied Sakura footage is catalogued stock. It illustrates a household situation; authentic product/label imagery establishes product identity. Cleaning efficacy, material compatibility and customer endorsement need their own evidence.

Use vn-natural-copy and current local RAG facts to write a concrete situation, one useful answer and a reason to call. Prepare three different opening mechanisms, then select the one the available footage can carry. Do not promise a demonstration the assets cannot show. Save DESIGN.md with the source intervals, chosen hook, payoff, attention target, framing and unresolved gaps. RAG grounds evidence; it does not train a content or voice model or establish a winning angle.

Protect faces, hands and the object carrying the story. Graphics should add a relationship or decision rather than repeat the entire caption. Preserve authentic labels and source attribution.
