# Vietnamese debate-led carousel tests

User direction, November 1: stronger controversial hooks to improve traffic. These are untested creative hypotheses, not proven winners. Preserve accepted posts and exact approved audio.

## Production prompt

Write a five-image Sakura story in natural everyday Vietnamese. Keep the current verified conditional offer visible, then challenge one recognisable household or shopping habit in the main hook. The next card must answer the tension rather than repeat a generic material checklist. Use the supplied reference photos, authentic Sakura500ml identity, distinct scenes and native frame review. Finish with the call-only CTA and current offer conditions. No internal codes, invented outcomes, fake customer quotations, personal accusations or unsupported product comparisons. Keep native AI and own-brand disclosures.

## Measurement

Record hypothesis, cover wording, offer cohort, music and format privately. Distinct music is user-required, so these organic tests cannot isolate the hook effect. Compare comparable24–72hour post ages, report distribution differences, and leave calls/orders unknown without observed attribution. More comments or controversy alone does not establish qualified leads. Never duplicate an accepted post for a hook change.
