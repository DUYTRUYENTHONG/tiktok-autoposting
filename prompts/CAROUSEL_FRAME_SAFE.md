# Reference-based photo carousel prompt

Use the latest campaign facts and reviewed source intent. Read the actual source photos before generation. A reference showing a person cleaning does not establish Sakura application, compatibility or a customer outcome.

## Reference order

1. A locally saved creative whose exact image bytes passed native Feed overlay review: layout and typography reference.
2. The authentic product photo: bottle geometry and label identity.
3. Optional scene reference: household context and original fictional actor only.

Putting a full-bleed scene first produced oversized banners and low offer conditions in the November1 window-track trial, despite numeric margin instructions. A passed layout first improved the next cover. This is an observed iteration, not a guarantee that later outputs pass.

## Prompt skeleton

Create one finished3:4 portrait Vietnamese TikTok photo card. Reference1 controls typography positioning and hierarchy. Reference2 controls authentic Sakura500ml product identity. The scene reference supplies household context only; do not reproduce unverified application or results.

Put the main message at top left, short lines, and its necessary conditions directly below in the upper middle. Start essential content inside x8–78%,y8–72%. Keep right22% and bottom25% background-only. Place product identity left-middle where the brand remains visible above lower overlays. Avoid full-width banners, footer conditions, tiny legal text and duplicated headlines. Use correct Vietnamese accents and the exact approved copy supplied for this card. Keep one meaningful beat per card and vary the photo framing. No public internal codes, workflow wording, invented claims or printed AI labels; native disclosures remain required.

## Acceptance

Inspect every actual returned file, not just its prompt. Record any moved/omitted condition, altered product label or overlapping text. Correct rejected outputs without overwriting them. After upload, manually swipe all five cards in the native Feed preview with actual caption, location and interaction overlays. Persist ordered hashes and screenshots. Generated or locally reviewed cards remain unsubmitted until the remaining account, music, offer, audience, disclosure and native-time checks pass.
