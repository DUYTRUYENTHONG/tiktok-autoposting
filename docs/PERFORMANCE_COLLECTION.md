# Collecting native performance evidence

Use the selected internal browser and the exact account. Reconcile publication receipts before attributing metrics. Read the account's own Studio content list, locate a known published post, and open its native analytics action. Verify the resulting post identity and caption before reading Overview. Loading placeholders, a generic success toast and a schedule are not publication or metric evidence.

The content list exposes views, likes and comments. Missing shares, saves or watch time in that table do not mean zero. The post Overview may expose additional counters, total play time, average watch time, full-video completion, new followers and a retention summary. Verify the labels/icons and exact displayed values; save a screenshot plus a text observation. Keep retention statements such as the most common stopping second separate from a numeric three-second retention rate. Do not invent a curve value from a chart or an error placeholder.

Record each observation's timestamp and timezone, exposed source URL, platform ID, format, actual publication time, product, organic/paid status and promotion cohort. Preserve native units and the raw text. Normalize a displayed completion percentage to a0–1fraction for `completionRate`. Keep unavailable traffic sources and processing notices missing. A traffic-source threshold observed on one post is not an account-wide guarantee. Viewer hours need an exposed timezone before they can inform scheduling; older durian activity does not establish cleaning-product behavior.

Import cumulative snapshots through `node src/cli.mjs import ABS_OBSERVATIONS.json`; never add successive cumulative counters together. Supported structured metrics include views, likes, comments, shares, saves, completionRate and retention3s when genuinely exposed. Store average watch seconds, native total play time, traffic percentages and the exact retention summary in a separate private observation receipt until a validated tracker field exists. Do not claim the dashboard displays or ingests a metric merely because a private file contains it.

Compare the same product, native format, promotion and organic/paid distribution at comparable24–72-hour observation ages. Record differences in hook, footage, duration, music and publication context. One post per angle cannot establish a winner. The kit's3-post/500-view thresholds are operating heuristics, not significance tests. Early drop-off supports testing opening relevance or clarity; it does not by itself isolate narration, music, visuals or audience mismatch as the cause.

Calls, qualified inquiries and orders require actual evidence. Leave them unknown when no observation exists. Never turn views, favorites or completion into leads. Preserve accepted posts; improve future unsubmitted creatives rather than deleting or duplicating a post to change its hook or sound. Close the task browser tabs after collection as requested.
